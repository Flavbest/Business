🏆 The 6 filters of a product worth testing

  • Solves a real problem or a clear desire (not "cool")
  • Evergreen — sells all year, not one season
  • Easy to film, with a visible "wow" moment in 2 seconds
  • Margin ≥ SAR 100 per unit after product + shipping
  • Few stores already pushing it in KSA (search TikTok in Arabic to check)
  • Small, light, no batteries/liquids/kids items (your customs rule)

📢 Ad rules from people who spend real money

  • Broad targeting, always. No narrow interests — the creative does the targeting, the algorithm finds the buyer.
  • The creative is 90% of results. Settings and hacks are 10%.
  • Volume wins: a real test = many variations. Aim for 4 angles × 5 hooks over time, not one video.
  • Don't starve an ad set. Under ~SAR 75/day per ad set teaches the algorithm nothing.

🤖 AI product-research prompt (paste into Claude)

Run this weekly. It thinks from pain points backward to products — better than browsing bestseller lists.

You are my product research engine for the Saudi e-commerce market.

Think from pain backward to product:
1. List 10 daily frustrations felt by people in Saudi Arabia across: home, beauty, pets, health, parents, car owners, office workers, hosting guests.
2. For each frustration, propose ONE physical product (available on Alibaba) that solves it instantly.
3. Only include products that are: emotionally painful problems, clear before/after, filmable wow moment, price SAR 80-300, margin over SAR 100, small and light, no batteries or liquids, and NOT already saturated on Saudi TikTok.

For each product give: name, the exact pain it solves, why it can win in KSA specifically, 3 video hook ideas in Arabic, estimated cost vs selling price, saturation score 1-10.

Finish with: your top 3 picks ranked, and a 20-second TikTok script in Saudi dialect for pick #1.

⚡ Store discipline

  • Fewer apps = faster store = more sales. Install only: reviews, order tracking, abandoned-cart WhatsApp.
  • Logo in 20 minutes on Canva. The logo never sold anything.
  • Never ship to customers directly from AliExpress — 3+ week delivery kills a Saudi store. Local stock or DDP bulk only.
